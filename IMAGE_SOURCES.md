# Product image sources

Product photos and source listings are mapped in [public/products/sources.json](public/products/sources.json). Most images are bundled under `public/products`; six verified product images are loaded from their listed manufacturer or retailer image URLs. Those require an internet connection when viewed. Several catalog labels were corrected to match the actual product shown, including Kwik’s Chicken Crackers, Wai Wai Quick Chicken Pizza, Kwik’s Hot & Spicy Potato Chips, Nebico D-20 Digestive Biscuits, Rato Bhale Sweet Lapsi Candy, and Nepal Foods Basmati Rice.
